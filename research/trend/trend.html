What we are harvesting

Alternative beta. Not alpha.

Time-series momentum is a published style premium. Sixty instruments, eight decades, every asset class. Expect 0.5 to 0.8 after cost. A Sharpe of 3 means a bug.

It pays because the payoff is unpleasant. Trend loses small and often in chop. It wins large and rarely in long moves. Few people want to hold that, so it is not competed away.

It earns its place on the regime map, not on Sharpe. Carry is short vol and pays in calm. Trend is long vol and pays in dislocation. Long-only crypto lost about 1.3 Sharpe in the 2022 bear. Trend earned about 0.9. Own both.

Crowding costs capacity, not edge. Capacity is not our constraint. Cost is.

Why it should be larger in crypto

Two amplifiers. Both mechanical, so both testable.

Forced deleveraging

Perps are levered and liquidate mechanically. A move against a crowded side triggers liquidations. Those push price the same way and trigger more. Trend monetizes that extension. If this is the source, trend is strongest when open interest is high going in.

Slow information diffusion

No earnings calendar, no central disclosure. News prices over days, not seconds. Under-reaction is the textbook trend mechanism and weekly cadence is sized to it.

Neither is tested. The test conditions book returns on open interest, which the source now carries.

The book

At each weekly close, take the trailing L-week return on every perp. Take its sign. Long if positive, short if negative. Size for equal risk. Hold a week. Re-decide.

How the per-symbol PnLs weight into a book is now a live decision. On ten mega-caps it was not.

Design decisions

Direction unverified

The whole signal. Sign of the trailing return, L swept over 1, 2, 4, 8 and 12 weeks. L=1 and L=4 cleared the bar on the old sample and everything between them did not. Winners whose neighbours disagree are lucky cells. Re-run the grid before building on it.

Size and weighting reopened

Target weekly vol per symbol, capped. Size does not move the hurdle, because fees and funding both scale with notional. It sets dollar PnL, drawdown and impact. Realized-vol scaling stays deferred. The vol estimate is a shared sizing utility, not a lane. A better one, EWMA or a GARCH family over the rolling std, is a coarse-first spike, does net Sharpe or drawdown move at all.

Weighting is what changed. Equal weight over ten mega-caps was a liquid book. Equal weight over four hundred perps puts BTC notional into a thin alt, which is maximum weight where the spread is widest and least known. Run equal weight and liquidity weight side by side. The gap between them is how much of the edge is a cost guess.

Cadence held

Weekly. A dead band cuts turnover but does not lift Sharpe. Daily lost across the grid. Give daily one cheap re-test, because the old sample resampled one-minute bars and the new source serves hourly.

Funding-aware sign unverified

A long in hot funding pays more than a small price move earns. Subtract trailing funding from the trailing return before taking the sign. This is what separates a directional premium from paying carry to be long crypto. The old lift was 0.06 and sat inside the noise. Re-test it.

Trend strength filter untested

The sign fires on any move. A 5% run and a 0.3% drift both take a full position. Scale the trailing return by its own volatility and act on the standardised move, so a weak name in chop sits smaller or flat and a clean run sizes up.

Two endpoints cannot tell a steady climb from a spike that round-tripped. Measure the path instead, efficiency ratio or the fit of price on time across the window, and gate on trend quality not just direction. Requiring several lookbacks to agree is the cheap version of the same idea.

The dead band was tested on turnover and came back null. This is the conviction question, whether a stronger or cleaner trend is a better trend, and it is untested.

Signal shape held

Continuous conviction, MA crossover, EWMA crossover and Baz-scored EWMA all lost to the plain sign. Crossovers sat at or below zero. Conviction lifted 0.22 in sample and nothing out of fold.

Combining lookbacks held

Winner-average, sawtooth-fitted and rolling-Sharpe combiners all failed out of sample. The sawtooth moves fold to fold. Reading it as signal chases luck.

The last two are held on thin evidence. Re-open them only if the plain lookback survives.

The bar

Unchanged on purpose. A bar rewritten after results is not a bar. All of it, out of sample, per year.

Stop condition

Written with the bar. If the wide sweep is flat across every cell net of the ladder, and no lookback clears 0.7 out of sample, pooled or inside any tested condition, close the lane. No third grid. No finer axis. No re-opened combiner.

Conditional pass

A strategy that misses the pooled bar can still pass. It has to clear the bar inside one market condition, consistently, out of sample. The condition must be known when the trade is placed. Define it before looking at returns, or fit it in fold. It must occur often enough to matter. Finding the condition is part of this gate. Switching on it live is a separate gate with its own model.

What we run next

00

Explore the data done

Trend is weak and lives at the market, 1.5 to 1.9 standard errors and a sign that flips by year. The stronger pattern is the opposite, coins snap back against the market over 1 to 2 weeks at 3.5 to 3.9. Coins under $10M a day show no trend at all. Returns are Student t near 3.3 degrees of freedom, so tail weeks are far more common than normal.

At $10M impact kills every book. Books thinned in 2026, $87k of depth for a $10M coin against $187k to $298k before, so recent cost is worse.

11

Signal shape, path and breakout

efficiency ratio, up-day fraction, R-squared of price on time, Donchian breakout, open-interest-confirmed break

The sign of two endpoints throws away the path. A steady grind and a spike that round-tripped read the same. Test whether a clean path predicts persistence better than the raw sign, and whether a new L-week high beats being above L weeks ago. Then back the break with capital, take it only when contract open interest is also near an L-week high, since a break on falling open interest is a squeeze that round-trips. Volume is the weaker read, it marks turnover not commitment and often marks exhaustion. Judge net lift over the plain sign out of fold, and expect it leverage-conditional. New, untested on this universe.

12

Signal decomposition

next-week return on market lags, residual lags, funding, open interest, walk-forward

Break the trailing return into its terms and fit the weight each earns out of fold. Splits into a weak market-trend term and the strong cross-sectional reversal term. The reversal term belongs to the cross-sectional lane. Subsumes the funding-aware sign and the boosted signal.

13

Statistical and positioning features

skewness, kurtosis, return autocorrelation, and the cross-sectional column, funding, open interest, dispersion, co-movement, liquidity, depth

The single-name price features are spent. Test the ones that carry independent information, higher moments and autocorrelation, autocorrelation being the trend against reversal question itself. Then the venue's real edge, the cross-sectional and positioning features, where forced deleveraging lives. Technical indicators are monotone transforms of price already carried, low prior, test cheap. Hypothesis first, then the smallest feature that expresses it, never a feature zoo sifted by a model. Judge net lift over the plain sign out of fold.

08 to 13 are exploratory, ML last and on hold. Fit walk-forward, judge on net lift over the plain sign out of fold, never on accuracy or IC. Deadband, conviction weighting and multi-horizon agreement were tested null on the old ten-symbol sample and get a wide re-test only if a shape in 11 moves first.

Where this stands

The streamed pipeline is verified. The wide sweep keeps the lane open but fails the pooled bar. The moderate-month condition is a parked candidate, real as a 98-week sleeve at even odds and under-powered to clear the search, judged on the sleeve a regime gate would trade rather than the always-on book. What it prices is a cross-sectional reversal, banked for the mean-reversion lane. That lane shares this one's universe, cadence and structure, so running the two together only repeats the same pipeline and findings. Trend runs to the end and is written up first, then mean reversion begins on top of it. This lane is pushed to the ends of the data, signal shape and backed breakout in 11 next.

Held. Simple beats complex, on every shape and combiner tested. Weekly beats daily.
