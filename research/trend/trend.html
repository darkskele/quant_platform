What we are harvesting

Time-series momentum is a documented style premium, not a private edge. It has been found across sixty instruments and eight decades, and it pays in equities, bonds, commodities and currencies. We are harvesting alternative beta and we should say so, because the framing sets what counts as a believable result. A Sharpe of 3 on a premium this well known would mean a bug, not a discovery. The 0.5 to 0.8 band is what a real trend premium looks like after cost, and that is the band to expect.

The premium is compensation for holding a payoff most participants do not want. Trend loses small and often in chop and wins large and rarely in extended directional moves. That shape is unpleasant to carry and hard to sit through, which is why it is not competed away despite being published.

Its place in the book is the regime map, not the headline Sharpe. Carry is short volatility and pays in calm. Trend is long volatility and pays in dislocation. On the prior sample long-only crypto lost about 1.3 Sharpe through the 2022 bear while trend earned about 0.9, because the trailing return was negative and the sign flipped short. Owning both is the whole argument for this lane.

What we are not claiming is a mispricing nobody else sees. This is crowded and public. Crowding costs capacity rather than edge, and capacity is not the binding constraint at our size. Cost is.

Why it should be larger in crypto

Two amplifiers are specific to perpetuals and both are mechanical rather than behavioural, which makes them testable instead of assumed.

Forced deleveraging

Perp positions carry leverage and liquidate mechanically. A move against a crowded side triggers liquidations that push price the same way, which triggers more. That is a reflexive extension of the original move and it is exactly the shape trend monetizes. If this is the source, trend should be strongest when open interest is high going into the move.

Slow information diffusion

There is no earnings calendar and no centralized disclosure, so information arrives unevenly and gets priced over days rather than seconds. Under-reaction of that kind is the textbook trend mechanism and the weekly cadence is sized to it.

Neither claim is tested yet. The test is conditioning book returns on open interest and on liquidation volume, and it is blocked, because the source carries klines, mark price, premium index and funding only. Open interest needs the metrics dataset and a new endpoint built for it.

The book

At each weekly close, look at the trailing L-week return on every perp in the universe. Take the sign, positive means go long, negative means go short. Size every position for equal risk contribution. Hold for a week, then re-decide. That is the whole strategy for one name, and the book is the equal-weight mean of the per-symbol PnLs.

Design decisions

Direction unverified

The whole signal. Sign of the trailing return over a chosen lookback, swept across 1, 2, 4, 8 and 12 weeks. On the prior sample L=1 and L=4 cleared the cost bar and everything between them did not. Neighbours disagreeing with both winners is the signature of a lucky cell, not a rewarded window, so the grid is re-run before anything is built on it.

Size held

Target weekly vol per symbol, capped. Sizing does not move the profit hurdle because fees and funding both scale with notional. It sets dollar PnL, drawdown and impact. Equal-weight for now, with realized-vol scaling deferred until a surviving signal is picked.

Cadence held

Weekly rebalance. A dead band that only flips the sign when the trailing return magnitude clears a threshold cuts turnover as designed but does not lift Sharpe. Daily cadence lost across the whole grid. Held rather than re-opened, though the wide run gets one cheap daily re-test because the prior sample resampled one-minute bars where the new source serves hourly natively.

Funding-aware sign unverified

A long in a hot-funding regime pays more than a small trailing price move earns. Subtracting trailing funding from the trailing return before taking the sign flips the marginal weeks that were only nominally trending up. Worth more than the prior 0.06 lift suggests, because it is the thing that separates the directional premium from simply paying carry to be long crypto beta. On four folds that lift sat inside the noise, so it is re-tested rather than assumed.

Signal shape held

Continuous conviction, fast-slow MA crossover, EWMA crossover and the Baz-scored vol-normalized EWMA all tested against the plain sign at L=1 on the same walk-forward. Every crossover shape hovered at or below zero. Conviction weighting showed a 0.22 in-sample lift that vanished out of fold. Plain sign won.

Combining lookbacks held

Naive winner-average, sawtooth-fitted and adaptive rolling-Sharpe combiners all failed to lift the book out of sample. The sawtooth is not a stable feature and its lookback signs move fold to fold, so a combiner that reads it as signal chases luck.

The last two are held on a thin sample, which is weak evidence for a null. They are held anyway because the premium literature says the same thing and because they only become worth re-opening if the plain lookback itself survives the wide run.

The bar

Unchanged from the original page on purpose. A bar rewritten after seeing results is not a bar. Profitable means all of the following hold, out of sample, per year:

Book Sharpe net of cost and funding > 0.7
Positive in every calendar year yes, incl. bear
Sign survives at 2x real cost yes
Fee + funding drag as fraction of gross < 40%
Beats long-only on the regime map bear-year positive

Proof protocol

00
Reproduce before extending. The old window through the new source must return the old number, or the pipeline is wrong and every wide result after it inherits the fault.
01
Five purged walk-forward folds. Parameters chosen on the early folds, headlines reported on the held-out ones, per year.
02
Every headline at three cost levels, zero, one times and two times the table. A sign that flips between 1x and 2x is not robust and gets dropped.
03
Deflated Sharpe accounts for the trial count. The lookback grid stays small and the whole grid's spread is what is reported, never the winner cell alone.
04
Per-year splits on every headline. 2024 does not carry a verdict and 2022 does not condemn one, every year counts.
05
Reference lines on every plot. Long-only unlevered, long-only vol-scaled, and one-week persistence. Trend has to beat these on the regime map, not on pooled Sharpe alone.
06
Universe membership is point in time. Listing dates and liquidity filters are as of the bar, never as of today, or the book knows which names survived.

Data

The old sample was ten symbols from 2022 to 2024, one-minute klines resampled to daily, and a cost table built from those same ten names. The backtest now streams the Binance Vision archive directly, which changes what is answerable and what is not.

What we gained is span and cross-section, roughly five times the history and forty times the names. Four test folds of thirty weeks becomes a sample that can tell a rewarded lookback from a lucky one, which is the entire reason the direction result is being re-run rather than trusted.

What we did not gain is cost, and that is now the weak link rather than the bars. Half spread needs depth or aggTrades and neither is in this source, so per-symbol cost stays unmeasurable until tier 2. The ten-name measurement of 14.9 bps round trip was taken on mega-caps and does not extend to a universe holding thin alts. Applying it flat across the book is the single most likely way this lane invents edge it does not have, so the wide run uses a spread ladder bucketed by trailing dollar volume, calibrated on the names that were measured and extrapolated by liquidity, declared as an assumption and stress-tested rather than believed.

Where this stands

Nothing on this page is currently verified on the new data. Two reads are held because the mechanism survives a change of sample even if the number does not, and four are open because they were measured on ten names over three years and that is not enough to separate a real effect from a fortunate fold.

Held. Simple beats complex, on every shape and every combiner tested. Weekly beats daily.

Open. The lookback grid and the 0.85 winner at L=1. The 14.9 bps cost. Positive in every calendar year, which was three observations. The funding-aware lift of 0.06, which sat inside four-fold noise.

What is not modelled offline

Slippage at book size on thin names, which needs depth and aggTrades and is the reason the cost ladder is an assumption rather than a measurement. Liquidation risk on the short leg in a squeeze, which is live-only. Regime coverage now reaches back to 2020, so it holds one deep bear and two bulls, but still no 2018-style disinflationary crash.
