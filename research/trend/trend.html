The book

At each weekly close, look at the trailing L-week return on every perp in the universe. Take the sign, positive means go long, negative means go short. Size every position for equal risk contribution. Hold for a week, then re-decide. That is the whole strategy for one name. The book is the equal-weight mean of the ten per-symbol PnLs.

What makes this a distinct source of return is the regime map. Long-only crypto lost about 1.3 Sharpe in the 2022 bear. Trend earned about 0.9 there, because the trailing return was negative and the sign flipped short. A book that owns both harvests both regimes.

The four decisions

Direction

The whole signal. Sign of the trailing return over a chosen lookback. Sweep L across 1, 2, 4, 8, 12 weeks to find which windows the market actually rewards. Both L=1 and L=4 clear the cost bar on the current sample.

Size

Target weekly vol per symbol, capped. Sizing does not change the profit hurdle because fees and funding both scale with notional. It sets dollar PnL, drawdown, and impact. The book uses equal-weight for now with realized-vol scaling deferred until the surviving signal is picked.

Cadence

Weekly rebalance is the default, low turnover keeps fee drag small. A daily-with-dead-band variant is the natural next lever, only flipping the sign when the trailing return crosses a threshold rather than every zero-crossing.

Funding-aware sign

A long in a hot-funding regime pays more than a small trailing price move earns. Subtracting the trailing funding from the trailing return before taking the sign flips the marginal weeks that were only nominally trending up. Lifts book Sharpe by about 0.06 at L=1 and 0.04 at L=4 on the current sample.

The bar

Written before runs so a result cannot be steered. Profitable means all of the following hold, out of sample, per year:

Book Sharpe net of cost and funding > 0.7
Positive in every calendar year yes, incl. bear
Sign survives at 2x real cost yes
Fee + funding drag as fraction of gross < 40%
Beats long-only on the regime map bear-year positive

Proof protocol

01
Five purged walk-forward folds. Parameters chosen on folds 1 to 3, headlines reported on folds 4 and 5, per year.
02
Every headline at three cost levels, zero, one times, two times the table. A sign that flips between 1x and 2x is not robust and gets dropped.
03
Deflated Sharpe accounts for the trial count. The lookback grid stays small, five cells is the first pass, and the whole grid's spread is what is reported, not the winner cell.
04
Per-year splits on every headline. 2024 does not carry a verdict and 2022 does not condemn one, both years count.
05
Reference lines on every plot. Long-only unlevered, long-only vol-scaled, and one-week persistence. Trend has to beat these on the regime map, not on pooled Sharpe alone.

Data

The current sample is ten symbols from 2022 to 2024, one-minute klines resampled to daily, 8-hour funding prints, and per-week half-spread and taker fee from the cost table. Every USD-M perp at one-hour bars from 2020, funding rates over the same span, and open interest are next, with the universe preserving listing dates so delisted names stay in.

What is not modelled offline

Slippage at book size on thin names, needs depth and aggTrades. Liquidation risk on the short leg in a squeeze, live-only. Regime coverage limited to the last three years of crypto, one bear and two bulls, so the sample does not include a 2018-style disinflationary crash.

Where this stands

L=1 book Sharpe 0.78 net of cost and funding, lifts to 0.85 with the funding-aware sign. L=4 lands at 0.61, lifts to 0.65. Both are positive in every calendar year, both keep their sign at 2x cost, and both beat long-only on the 2022 regime. The soft part is the lookback grid, a sawtooth of two winners with negative neighbours, on three years of data.
